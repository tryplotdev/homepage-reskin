Colour

The hue is the group. Trending, More posts, and Engagement up are all green because they are all Momentum. Purple is Unique, blue is Engagement, red is Sentiment, and grey is Context. The word says which rule fired.

Orange, teal, pink, and maroon are spare. They are not assigned, so a new group can take a new hue instead of sharing one.
