Oct 26 sync · Label system

What a card is allowed to say

A card prints one label. If several are true, the stronger one prints. The rest stay off the card. The color is the group. Hover a label to read what it means.

Groups

Momentum

Something is happening now. A peak this week, or a jump today.

Unique

This does not look like the usual pattern.

Engagement

A reaction is high for its bucket, not just because the post was seen a lot.

Sentiment

The tone is negative.

Context

Who made it. Useful, and not a reason to open the card.

The order

If several are true, the earlier one prints. Standout already means the post is unusual, so Unusual does not print beside it. Engagement labels stay separate: a post can be widely shared without being the most viewed, because each one is a rate inside its bucket.
