Unique cards

src/components/unique-card.tsx · unique-row.tsx

Row rule
Show a unique row only when it has 3 posts. One or two posts never render. Tombstones still count as part of the 3 until the dismiss is submitted.
Layout
One column until 1024px, two columns from 1024px, three columns from 1280px.
Title
Good Sans Medium, 28px, line-height 33px, tracking -0.56px. Do not truncate. In a row, title boxes stretch to the tallest so the videos line up.
Post card
The video is the shared post card. 9:16, object-contain, no crop. Card and video radius are both 8px. Max width 193.5px, padding 9px.
Chrome
Plain “Unique Post” label, 12px, #8d8d8d. No pill. Grainy background. Cards tilt +1.58° and -1.58°, alternating.
Actions
Ask AI sits 16px below the image. Dark sparkle at 16px. Thumbs up is feedback. Thumbs down replaces that card with a tombstone.

Contextual chat

src/components/ask-chat/ask-chat.tsx

Dock
Ask AI on a card or the header sparkle opens this panel on the right. It is 380px wide and the full viewport height. The page shifts left. The chat does not cover the feed. Below 720px the width is the smaller of 380px and the viewport minus 72px.
Story
A card attaches that theme or unique post as a chip, plus Feed Stories. The header sparkle opens the same panel with only the feed chip.
Prompts
With a story, the title is “What insights are we after today?” and the suggestions are about that story. Without one, the title is “Refine your feed.” Clicking a suggestion sends it.
Composer
The story chip can be removed. Send stays off until there is text. Enter sends.
Close
The X dismisses the dock. New chat clears the thread and puts the chips back. On this specimen, X resets the panel so it stays on the page.

Daily picks quiz

src/components/preferences-quiz.tsx

Placement
Replaces the gray daily picks unit. Same width, in that slot. It does not jump to the top of the page. The Daily Picks title and header buttons stay.
Steps
Four questions: focus, topics, performance signal, languages. Multi-select.
Next
Disabled until at least one choice on questions 1–3. Question 4 is always enabled. Back and Next sit under the question.
Skip
Only on questions 3 and 4. Question 3 advances with nothing selected. Question 4 clears languages and saves.
Close
The corner X is the banner close: 28px, 8px radius, rgba(35, 6, 3, 0.06), 11px gray mark. Back on question 1 also closes and discards unsaved edits.
Languages
The field is white. The menu overlays under the field, 320px wide and 180px tall, and does not change the card height.
